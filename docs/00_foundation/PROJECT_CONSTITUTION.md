# Project Constitution

Version: 1.0  
Status: Approved  
Project: Project Hifz Rehberi  
Product: Hifz Rehberi

## 1. Purpose

This constitution defines the binding product, design, development and
AI-assistance principles of Project Hifz Rehberi.

It is the highest-level internal authority for all project work.

## 2. Product Identity

Hifz Rehberi is a digital companion for structured and traditional
Qur'an memorization.

The application supports students, teachers, parents and institutions
throughout the Hifz journey.

Technology supports the traditional learning process. It does not replace
qualified teachers, religious responsibility or human guidance.

## 3. Traditional Foundation

Traditional Hifz methods remain the primary foundation of the product.

Digital functions may:

- structure the learning process,
- support repetition,
- document progress,
- improve organization,
- provide reminders,
- support listening and recitation practice.

Digital functions must not weaken or replace the traditional relationship
between student and teacher.

## 4. Religious Reliability

Religious content must come from reliable and explicitly approved sources.

Approved source categories include:

- Diyanet,
- sources explicitly approved by the CEO,
- additional reliable sources reviewed before integration.

No AI agent may independently create, reinterpret or publish binding
religious content.

## 5. Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence may support:

- planning,
- pronunciation practice,
- recitation analysis,
- mistake detection,
- repetition recommendations,
- software development,
- quality assurance.

AI must never be presented as a replacement for a qualified teacher.

AI-supported recitation and mistake detection should strive toward a
high-quality standard comparable to leading specialist applications.

## 6. Design Identity

The product must feel:

- calm,
- respectful,
- elegant,
- trustworthy,
- modern,
- rooted in traditional Hifz culture.

The binding core colors are:

- emerald,
- turquoise,
- gold,
- ivory.

The original approved Hifz Rehberi logo is binding.

## 7. Product Governance

The CEO retains final authority over:

- product direction,
- scope,
- design approval,
- branding,
- religious sources,
- sprint approval,
- releases.

AI agents and developers may make recommendations but may not silently
change approved decisions.

## 8. Scope Discipline

During an active phase or sprint, no new concept may be introduced unless:

- it is necessary for the current milestone, or
- it provides significant long-term value to the application.

Such concepts must only be presented as proposals.

The CEO decides whether they are:

- accepted now,
- added to the backlog,
- rejected.

## 9. Quality Principle

Every implementation must be reviewed for:

- product intent,
- religious sensitivity,
- brand consistency,
- maintainability,
- testability,
- real-device behavior,
- sprint scope.

## 10. Change Control

This constitution is frozen as version 1.0.

Future changes require:

1. an explicit proposal,
2. CEO approval,
3. a documented version increment,
4. a dedicated Git commit.
