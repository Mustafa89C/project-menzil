# Product Foundation

Version: 1.0  
Status: Approved

## 1. Product

Product name: Hifz Rehberi  
Internal project name: Project Hifz Rehberi  
Platform foundation: Flutter and Dart

## 2. Core Problem

Students frequently struggle with:

- organizing new memorization,
- maintaining repetition,
- identifying weak pages,
- tracking mistakes,
- preparing regular submissions,
- staying motivated,
- continuing effectively without daily teacher access.

Teachers and institutions frequently struggle with:

- monitoring multiple students,
- documenting submissions,
- identifying delays,
- tracking repetition quality,
- maintaining a clear class overview.

## 3. Product Solution

Hifz Rehberi creates one structured environment for:

- memorization planning,
- repetition planning,
- submission tracking,
- mistake tracking,
- progress visualization,
- teacher supervision,
- parent visibility where appropriate,
- institutional organization,
- supportive AI functions.

## 4. Primary Users

### Student

The student memorizes, repeats, listens, records progress and prepares
submissions.

### Teacher

The teacher supervises students, reviews submissions, records mistakes and
guides the learning process.

### Parent

The parent may receive an appropriate overview of progress and consistency.

### Institution

The institution manages classes, teachers, students and learning structures.

### Mentor

A future supporting role that may assist learners without replacing the
qualified teacher.

## 5. Learning Methods

The product must support traditional and structured methods, including:

### Traditional Turkish-Ottoman Method

The student begins with the final page of each Juz, then continues with the
previous page in later rounds until the Juz is completed.

### 10-10-10 Method

The Qur'an is organized into three groups:

- final ten Juz,
- middle ten Juz,
- first ten Juz.

### Individual Method

A flexible method configured according to the learner, teacher or
institution.

Additional methods require explicit approval before implementation.

## 6. Core Learning Rules

The detailed learning configuration may include:

- study days per week,
- next-day submission,
- mistake limits per page,
- repetition after an unsuccessful submission,
- mandatory review in a later round,
- page-based progress,
- time spent on a page,
- submission history.

Example approved mistake framework:

- first round: maximum five mistakes per page,
- later rounds: maximum three mistakes per page.

These values must remain configurable where required by the teacher or
institution.

## 7. Core Functional Areas

- Start and onboarding
- Student dashboard
- Teacher dashboard
- Reading and Mushaf mode
- Memorization planning
- Repetition planning
- Submission management
- Mistake documentation
- Audio listening and recording
- Progress and motivation
- Class and institution management
- AI-supported learning assistance

## 8. Registration Principle

Reading, listening, Tajwid and Tafsir content may be accessible without
registration where legally and technically appropriate.

Personal progress, saved markings, learning plans and account-specific data
require registration.

## 9. Privacy Principle

The application must avoid unnecessary data collection.

Audio recordings must only be stored for the duration required by the
approved feature and privacy concept.

The product should not rely on advertising.

## 10. Language Principle

Initial product languages:

- German,
- Turkish.

Additional languages may be introduced later.

## 11. Business Principle

The product should remain affordable for individual learners.

Possible models include:

- low-cost individual access,
- regionally adjusted pricing,
- institutional licensing,
- premium teacher or institution functions.

Final prices require a separate approved business decision.

## 12. Product Success

Hifz Rehberi succeeds when it:

- improves consistency,
- strengthens repetition,
- reduces forgotten pages,
- helps teachers supervise effectively,
- protects traditional learning values,
- remains calm and simple to use,
- earns the trust of learners and institutions.
